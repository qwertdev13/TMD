Summary of the Current Venezuelan Oil Crisis and U.S. Policy

The ongoing situation in Venezuela has intensified under the Trump administration, marked by a controversial approach to foreign policy that prioritizes American interests, particularly in the oil sector. President Trump's rhetoric portrays Venezuela as a hotspot for drug cartels, specifically targeting the so-called **Cartel de los Soles**, allegedly led by President Nicolás Maduro. Critics argue that this alleged cartel serves more as a catch-all label for corrupt officials rather than a structured organization.

Leftist Perspective

Left-leaning commentators assert that the U.S. government's focus on Venezuela reflects a modern land grab motivated by the desire to control the country's vast oil reserves, which are the largest in the world. They argue that Trump’s interventionist policies aim not only to destabilize the region but also to undermine energy deals involving China, Iran, and Russia. Venezuelan leadership, including Maduro, claims that U.S. military buildup in the region — consisting of **15,000 troops, aircraft carriers, and guided-missile destroyers** — indicates an outright effort to seize control of its oil resources.
